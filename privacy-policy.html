PawClick Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 August 2026

PawClick ("the app") is a pet training clicker app. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: the app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

No accounts. No analytics. No advertising. No servers.
PawClick has no backend. Nothing you do in the app leaves your device.

Information stored on your device

The app saves the following locally, using Android's app storage, so your setup survives between sessions:

  • Your settings (selected sound, volume, boost, pitch, vibration, theme, whistle frequency)
  • Pet profiles you create (the name and species you type in)
  • Training counters (clicks per pet, daily totals, streak)
  • Your recorded custom marker sound, if you choose to record one

This information stays on your device. It is not uploaded, backed up to our systems, or shared with anyone. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

Microphone

PawClick requests microphone access for one optional feature: recording your own marker sound (for example, saying "Yes!"). Recording only happens while you actively press the record button. The resulting audio is stored on your device and used only to play back your marker sound. It is never transmitted anywhere. If you never use this feature, the app never accesses your microphone, and you may deny the permission with no effect on any other feature.

Purchases

PawClick Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed entirely by Google Play Billing. Google handles the transaction and your payment details; we never see or store your payment information. The app only receives a signal indicating whether the Pro product is owned by your Google account. Google's handling of purchase data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Data we collect

None. PawClick collects no personal data, no usage analytics, no crash reports, no device identifiers, and no location data. It contains no advertising SDKs and no third-party tracking libraries.

Children

PawClick is not directed at children under 13. Because the app collects no data at all, it collects no data from children either.
